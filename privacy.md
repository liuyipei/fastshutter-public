# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: March 29, 2026*

## Overview

FastShutter is a camera app that saves photos and videos directly to your device's photo library. Your privacy is straightforward: we don't collect, store, or transmit your data.

## Data Collection

FastShutter does not collect any personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.

## Camera and Microphone

FastShutter requests camera access to function. Microphone access is requested only if you explicitly enable audio for video recording. These permissions are handled entirely by iOS and can be revoked at any time in Settings.

## Photo Library

Photos and videos are saved directly to your device's photo library using iOS system APIs. FastShutter does not upload, transmit, or process your media in any way.

## Network

FastShutter makes no network requests. The app functions entirely offline.

## In-App Purchases

Premium features are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. FastShutter does not process or store any payment information.

## Contact

If you have questions, please [open an issue](https://github.com/liuyipei/fastshutter-public/issues).
